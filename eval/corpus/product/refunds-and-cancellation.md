# Refunds and Cancellation

## Cancelling a subscription
Customers can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. Monthly plans stay active until the end of the current billing month and are not refunded for the remaining days. Annual plans can be cancelled at any time; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid term.

## Refund window
New customers on an annual plan can request a full refund within 30 days of the first payment. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded pro rata only when Northwind fails to meet the uptime commitment for two consecutive months. Monthly plans are not eligible for refunds except where required by law.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Refunds for bank transfers are paid to the account that sent the original payment. Customers receive an email confirmation with the refund reference number.

## Downgrades
Downgrading from Team to Starter takes effect at the next renewal date. Projects above the Starter limit become read-only until the number of active projects is reduced to 3 or fewer.
