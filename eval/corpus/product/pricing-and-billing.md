# Pricing and Billing

## Plans
Northwind Cloud offers three plans. Starter costs 29 USD per month and includes 3 projects and 50 GB of storage. Team costs 99 USD per month and includes 20 projects, 500 GB of storage and single sign-on. Enterprise is priced per contract and includes unlimited projects, a dedicated support engineer and a 99.95% uptime commitment.

## Annual billing
Customers who pay annually receive a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. Annual plans are invoiced up front at the start of the subscription term. Seats added during an annual term are prorated to the end of the current term.

## Usage overages
Storage above the plan allowance is billed at 0.08 USD per GB per month. Egress bandwidth above 1 TB per month is billed at 0.05 USD per GB. Overage charges appear on the next invoice after the month in which they were incurred.

## Payment methods
Monthly plans are paid by credit card. Annual Team and Enterprise plans can also be paid by bank transfer with net-30 payment terms. Invoices are issued in USD, EUR or GBP based on the billing address.

## Failed payments
If a card payment fails, Northwind retries the charge after 3 days and again after 7 days. If all retries fail, the account enters a 14-day grace period during which the service keeps running. After the grace period the account is suspended until payment is received; data is retained for 60 days after suspension.
