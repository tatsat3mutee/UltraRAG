# Service Level Agreement and Support

## Uptime commitment
Northwind commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Team plans and 99.95% for Enterprise plans. Starter plans have no uptime commitment. Uptime is measured by external probes every minute against the public API.

## Service credits
If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee for uptime below the commitment but at or above 99.0%, and 25% of the monthly fee for uptime below 99.0%. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month and are applied to the next invoice. Credits cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Support channels and response times
Starter customers get community forum support and email support with a first response within 2 business days. Team customers get email and chat support with a first response within 8 business hours. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support with a first response within 1 hour for severity-1 incidents, where production is down.

## Maintenance windows
Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC and is announced at least 7 days in advance on the status page. Planned maintenance does not count as downtime.
