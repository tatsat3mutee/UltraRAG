# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly at 2 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than three days. Managers approve or decline requests within 5 working days.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Days above the carry-over limit are forfeited unless a written exception is approved by the People team because a business need prevented the employee from taking leave.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick absence of more than 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on exit. Employees should notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year after birth or adoption. Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible.

## Public holidays
Northwind observes the public holidays of the country where the employee's contract is held. If a public holiday falls during annual leave, that day is not deducted from the leave balance.
