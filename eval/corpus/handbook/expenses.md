# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses
All business expenses must be submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date, with an itemised receipt attached. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Approval limits
Expenses up to 500 USD are approved by the employee's direct manager. Expenses between 500 and 5,000 USD also require approval from the department head. Anything above 5,000 USD requires CFO approval before the purchase is made.

## Travel
Flights under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. Business class is not reimbursed unless approved in advance by the CFO. Hotels are reimbursed up to 220 USD per night in standard cities and up to 350 USD per night in high-cost cities such as London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich.

## Meals and per diem
When travelling, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 75 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment is reimbursed up to 120 USD per attendee and requires the names and companies of all attendees on the claim.

## Home office
Each employee may claim a one-time home office allowance of 600 USD for a desk, chair or monitor, and a monthly internet stipend of 40 USD.
