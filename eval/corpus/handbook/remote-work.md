# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Eligibility
Most roles at Northwind can be performed remotely. Employees can work from any location within their contracted country. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a 12-month period requires approval from the People team because of tax and immigration rules.

## Core hours
Teams agree their own schedules, but everyone should be available for collaboration during core hours of 11:00 to 15:00 in their team's primary time zone. Meetings outside core hours should be optional or recorded.

## Office access
Offices in Berlin, Austin and Singapore are open on weekdays from 08:00 to 20:00. Desks are booked through Robin. Each team holds an in-person planning week once per quarter, and travel for it is covered by the company.

## Equipment
Remote employees receive the same laptop and peripherals as office employees. Equipment must be returned within 10 working days of leaving the company using the prepaid shipping kit.
