# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
All company accounts must use single sign-on with hardware security keys. Passwords for systems outside single sign-on must be at least 14 characters and stored in the company password manager, 1Password. Passwords must never be shared over chat or email.

## Devices
Laptops are issued with full-disk encryption enabled and are managed through Kandji. Devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery so they can be wiped remotely.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is always Restricted. Restricted data may only be stored in approved production systems and must never be copied to laptops, personal cloud storage or AI tools that are not on the approved vendor list.

## Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails that were clicked, must be reported in the #security-incidents channel immediately. The security on-call engineer acknowledges reports within 15 minutes during business hours and within 30 minutes outside business hours.

## Access reviews
Access to production systems is reviewed every quarter by each system owner. Access that is not re-approved during the review is revoked automatically 7 days after the review closes.
